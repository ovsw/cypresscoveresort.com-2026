# Concept A1 homepage — Gold & Green (EXPERIMENTAL, superseded)

Status: first, experimental version, built with Impeccable 4.5 on 2026-11-13. Frozen for reference. Its direction was picked without dealing alternatives. Concept A2 replaces it and does not follow this contract.

Scope: frozen copy `design/concept-a1.html` with `design/css/a1/` (originally built on `design/index.html` + `design/css/theme-a.css`). Mode: Persuade. Desktop only (1440) in this round; no mobile work, by Ovi's instruction on 2026-11-13. Shared markup and `base.css` stay untouched so Concept B compares like for like.

Audience and job: people who don't come yet (nervous first-timers, naturists comparing resorts). Believe "a real resort that happens to be nudist"; act on Book Now, then tour, then day pass. Proof: ratings, the comparison, reviews, photos.

Constraints: section order, copy and nav are approved. No pastels, no tilted elements, no carousels, no countdowns. Body sans stays geometric. Real resort photos only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A 1964 Florida resort brochure, printed in two inks. Colour stays quiet and expensive; the italic does the dancing. Refuses the campground look and the pastel "island" palette.

OWN-WORLD: Cypress green fields (#153B2C) and cream paper (#F4EFE1), pale sage bands, one brass gold used for italics, hairlines, numerals and the action on green. Libre Caslon Text, roman with gold italic emphasis; Jost for reading. Square corners, 1px gold hairlines, a gold keyline inset in the large photos, photos graded warm and slightly muted.

STORY: It is a real resort; here is the proof; other guests were nervous too; here is how a first visit works; book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green header and hero as one field. Left: three-line Caslon headline about 76px, "resort." in gold italic, lead, gold Book Now and outline "Plan your first visit", then the 4.7 Tripadvisor line. Right: the lake photo, flush under the header, bleeding off the right edge and dropping about 80px into the cream section below, gold keyline inside, a cream caption tab "Kissimmee, Florida" on its lower-left corner.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (Concept A in docs/design-brief.md); no roll, no seed key. Code-led: no image generation on this machine. Signature motion: the hero photo unveils left to right once on load; keylines ease outward on hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Open: DESIGN.md is held until the client picks A, B or a mix (a root DESIGN.md now would bind Concept B to A's look). Palette, type and buttons are recorded in `design/concept-a.html`.
