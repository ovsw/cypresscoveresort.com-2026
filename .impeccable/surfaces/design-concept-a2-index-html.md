# Concept A2 homepage — Gold & Green, "Golden Hour"

Scope: `design/concept-a2/` (index.html, styles.css, notes.html). Mode: Persuade. Desktop only, 1440px, by the brief. Chosen by Ovi on the decision page, 2026-11-13, from three dealt directions (Golden Hour, Cabana Club, Gilt Lettering). Replaces the frozen Concept A1; shares nothing with it or with Concept B but the photos in `design/img/`.

Audience and job: people who don't come yet (nervous first-timers, naturists comparing resorts). Believe "a real resort that happens to be nudist"; act on Book Now, then a tour, then a day pass. Proof: ratings, the comparison, real guest reviews, photos.

Constraints: section order, copy and navigation are client-approved. Green, gold and cream only; no pastels. Playful headings with italic emphasis; geometric sans for reading. The brief's avoid list is hard: nothing tilted, no carousels, no countdowns, no illustrative icons.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one day at the Cove: dusk on the lake, a clear morning for the facts, evening for the events, night for where you sleep, dawn for the first-visit offer. Gold is only ever light. Refuses the split hero and the cream-paper brochure of A1.

OWN-WORLD: Two inks. Greens from night (#0A1F19) and dusk (#0C241D) through cypress (#12332A) to pale sage; cream (#F6EFDD) is daylight; gold (#E3B55B, #8C6414 on cream) marks light and the one action. Petrona roman with gold italic emphasis; Figtree for reading. Flat fields, hard edges, no shadows, one 12-column grid, square-edged photos at full scale.

STORY: It is a real resort; here is the proof; other guests were unsure too; here is what's on and where you sleep; a first visit is easy; book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The dusk lake photo fills the screen. Headline bottom left on the water at 116px, "For Nudists." in gold italic. One gold horizon line edge to edge; under it the lead, the 4.7 Tripadvisor rating, gold Book Now and outline "Plan your first visit". A gold sun on a hairline at the right edge.

FORM: Candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:db7703168ccf84af. Signature motion: the sun sinks behind the horizon line as the hero scrolls; a string of lights comes on over the evening section; the gold dawn band opens at the offer.
