# Concept B homepage — Island Colours, "House Colours"

Scope: `design/concept-b/` (index.html, styles.css, notes.html). Mode: Persuade. Desktop only, 1440px, by the brief. Chosen by Ovi on the decision page, 2026-11-13, from three dealt directions (The Plates, Porch & Shutters, House Colours). Shares nothing with Concept A2 or the frozen A1 but the photos in `design/img/`.

Audience and job: people who don't come yet (nervous first-timers, naturists comparing resorts). Believe "a real resort that happens to be nudist"; act on Book Now, then a tour, then a day pass. Proof: ratings, the comparison, real guest reviews, photos.

Constraints: section order, copy and navigation are client-approved. Green base plus the soft building yellow, blue and pink; no gold. Refined headings with no playful emphasis; geometric sans for reading. The brief's avoid list is hard: nothing tilted, no carousels, no countdowns, no illustrative icons, no primary colours.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each building colour gets one job on the page, as each building has one colour on the grounds; green is the path between them. Refuses the photo-under-centred-headline resort hero and decoration by colour.

OWN-WORLD: Flat, square-edged colour fields on a cream page (#FBF7EE): yellow #F3DE95 for staying, blue #BCD9E1 for water and daytime, pink #F2C4BA for evenings and events, sage #CFE2C8 for the gate and the first visit; deep green #1B4636 for text, actions and the footer. Ovo, upright only, for headings; Kumbh Sans for reading. No shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners; photos as wide bands and large straight tiles.

STORY: It is a real resort; here is the proof; other guests were unsure too; here is what's on and where you sleep; a first visit is easy; book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cream page. Headline top left at 94px in deep green; lead, Book Now and "Plan your first visit" top right, the 4.7 Tripadvisor line above them. The lake photo runs edge to edge as a 300px band. At the fold the four proof points start as a row of four colour fields: yellow, blue, pink, sage.

FORM: Candidate 2 of 7 on the ordered list (dealt beside the assigned direction; Ovi picked it); seed:c953aa50a8867db5. Code-led: no image generation on this machine; the picked sketch `.impeccable/mocks/decision/b-house-colours.html` is the composition. Signature interaction: the four menu groups carry the four colours (First Visit sage, Stay yellow, Play blue, Events pink), and the four fields rise in sequence once on load.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
