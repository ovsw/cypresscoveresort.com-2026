Concept A2, Gold & Green

Premium colours, playful type. Palette, type and buttons for the homepage design in index.html. Desktop, 1440px.

How it is fun and festive, and premium

  • One day at the Cove. The page runs from dusk on the lake to a clear morning for the facts, evening for the events, night for where you sleep, and dawn for the first-visit offer.
  • Premium is the colour. Two inks only: cypress greens and gold, with cream as daylight. Flat fields, hard edges, and photographs at full scale.
  • Fun and festive is the type and the details. Petrona's italic on a word or two per heading, a string of lights over the events, and a sun that sets as you scroll.
  • Gold is only ever light. It marks the sun, the lights and the one action on screen, so Book Now is always the brightest thing in view.
  • Easy on older eyes. 18px Figtree for reading, sturdy serif headings from 32px up, and AA contrast on every ground.
