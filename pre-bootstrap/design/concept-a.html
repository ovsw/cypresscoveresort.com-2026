Concept A: Gold & Green

  • Premium comes from the colour: cypress green, cream and pale sage, with one brass gold. No pastels.
  • Fun and festive comes from the type: Libre Caslon Text, with a word or two per heading in a gold, dancing italic.
  • Big photos do the rest: a hero photo that runs off the page edge, a gold keyline inside the large pictures, one warm grade across all of them.
  • Kept warm, not corporate: cream paper instead of white, italic captions and signatures, gold on every main action on green.
