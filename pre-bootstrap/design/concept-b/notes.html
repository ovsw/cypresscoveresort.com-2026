Why it works

  1. Fun and festive comes from colour. The four soft building colours arrive as a row on the first screen, then each owns one part of the page: yellow for staying, blue for the lake and the pools, pink for the evenings, sage for the first visit.
  2. Premium comes from type and space. Ovo, a calm classical serif, set large and upright with no playful emphasis; wide margins, square edges, big photographs, one idea per screen.
  3. It stays clear of "summer camp". The colours are soft and blended, and a colour never appears as decoration. After the opening row, colour arrives one field at a time; only the couples and families pair sets two side by side.
  4. It is a Key West resort. The colours are the ones the resort painted on its buildings in 2026, so the site and the grounds match the day a guest arrives.
